### 5. The Process — Our CRO process
- Eyebrow/label: `The Process`
- H2: `Our CRO process`
- Intro: `Six stages, then it loops. Each cycle typically runs four to six weeks depending on traffic volume.`
- 6 steps:
  1. `01` — `Research` — `Analytics, session recordings, heatmaps, on-site search and customer feedback. We want to know what people are trying to do and where it breaks, before anyone has an opinion about button colour.`
  2. `02` — `Audit` — `A structured walk-through of every step from landing to purchase, on mobile first. Every friction point logged with the evidence behind it.`
  3. `03` — `Identify Opportunities` — `Findings turned into testable hypotheses: what we think is happening, what we would change, and what metric should move if we are right.`
  4. `04` — `Prioritise` — `Scored on expected impact, traffic volume on the affected page and implementation effort. High-traffic, high-friction, low-effort goes first.`
  5. `05` — `Test & Implement` — `A/B tests where traffic supports significance, and evidence-led direct implementation where it does not. Both are documented the same way.`
  6. `06` — `Measure & Optimize` — `Result recorded either way, winners rolled out and monitored for decay, losers written up so nobody retries them in eighteen months.`
- Quirk: British spelling `Prioritise` here (and `prioritised` in the hero/meta), while section 3 uses `Prioritized Testing` in section 9 — kept verbatim.
