  6. Q: `Who writes the product descriptions?` A: `Our copywriters, working from your specs, review data and the search terms people actually use.   7. Q: `Do you produce video?` A: `Short-form product video, ad cuts, animation and motion graphics, yes — the formats paid social and PDPs consume. Product content at catalog scale depends on volume and how complete your attribute data is. Rush work is possible and we will say what it costs rather than quietly deprioritising something else to absorb it.`
