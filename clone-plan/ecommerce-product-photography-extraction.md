### 9. FAQs
- Eyebrow/label: `FAQs`
- H2: `Product photography FAQs`
- Intro: `Eight questions that come up before most photography engagements. More on our full FAQ page.` — Link: `our full FAQ page` → /faqs/
- 8 FAQs (also in JSON-LD FAQPage, verbatim):
  1. Q: `What exactly is included in product photography services?` A: `Six production lines: hero and catalogue frames, lifestyle sets, detail and macro, scale and fit imagery, 360 spins and short-form video, and infographic or A+ modules. Every engagement also includes the gallery audit, the shot list, retouching and a per-channel export set. What you will not get is a folder of raw files and a goodbye.`
  2. Q: `Do you shoot in a studio or on location?` A: `Both, and the choice is driven by the product rather than by convenience. Hero, detail and 360 work belongs in a controlled studio set-up. Lifestyle and in-context sets usually do not, because a room that looks like a room outperforms a room that looks like a set.`
  3. Q: `How does product photography pricing work?` A: `Per SKU, against a frame count agreed in the shot list, with the rate stepping down as volume rises. Product photography pricing quoted as a day rate hides the number that matters, which is cost per finished, channel-ready image. Send a SKU list and we will quote on that basis; our pricing page explains how engagements are structured more generally.` (Link inside answer: `our pricing page` → /pricing/)
  4. Q: `Do we ship you product, or do you come to us?` A: `Ship to us for studio work — it is cheaper and the lighting is already dialled in. We travel for lifestyle sets, oversized items, anything that cannot safely be couriered, and for on-model apparel work where the fit has to be checked live.`
  5. Q: `How long does a shoot take end to end?` A: `Five to seven business days from approved brief to final files for a standard set, assuming product is with us and the sample SKU is signed off promptly. Large catalogues run as cycles rather than as one long shoot, so you get usable files in batches instead of waiting for the whole run.`
  6. Q: `Who owns the images and the usage rights?` A: `You do, outright. Full-resolution masters plus every channel export, with usage rights assigned to you on delivery — no licence window, no per-channel surcharge, no re-licensing when you want to run the same shot as an ad.`
  7. Q: `Can you match our existing imagery?` A: `Yes, and on a mixed catalogue it is usually the right call — a gallery where half the SKUs were shot differently reads as careless. We match lighting, angle, crop and background from your existing set, and we will say plainly if the existing look is the thing holding the page back.`
  8. Q: `Do you handle Amazon A+ and marketplace modules too?` A: `Yes. A+ content, Walmart rich media and marketplace infographics come out of the same shoot rather than being commissioned separately, which is the main reason marketplace modules usually look like they belong to a different brand. Our Amazon team briefs them.` (Link inside answer: `Amazon team` → /amazon-agency/)
