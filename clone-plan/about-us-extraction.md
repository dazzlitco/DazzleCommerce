- H2: How it all started
- P: In 2017 our founder was running digital for a nine-figure home goods retailer and cycling through agencies at an embarrassing rate.
- P: The pattern never changed. A senior partner would win the pitch, promise strategic partnership, and then hand the account to someone three months out of university. Reporting arrived as a deck full of impressions. Nobody could answer the only question that mattered: is this channel making us money after costs?
- P: So she left and started the agency she had been trying to hire. Two people, one Shopify client, and a rule that has survived every stage of growth since: the person who sells the work does the work.
- P: Nine years later that rule still constrains how fast we grow. We cap the number of accounts each strategist carries, which means we turn away business most quarters. It is the least scalable thing about us and the reason clients stay an average of 31 months.

- H3: How big is the team and who will I work with?
- P: Forty-two specialists across strategy, marketing, operations, engineering, retention and creative. You get a named senior strategist who owns the relationship plus a pod of specialists for your active channels. That strategist is on every call.
- H3: Are you a full-service eCommerce agency or a specialist?
- P: Both, deliberately. Full-service in coverage, but each discipline is run by a specialist rather than a generalist. What we are not is a general digital agency that also does eCommerce.
- H3: What size of business do you typically work with?
- P: Most clients run between $500K and $30M in annual online revenue. We do have a lighter engagement for smaller stores focused on one or two channels.
- H3: Do you work with businesses outside the United States?
- P: Yes. Roughly 40% of clients are outside the US, mainly in the UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE. We staff across five time zones so account calls happen in your working hours.
- H3: What makes you different from the last agency we hired?
- P: Three things clients tell us repeatedly: the senior strategist genuinely stays on the account, we report contribution margin instead of only ROAS, and the agreement is month-to-month so we have to keep earning it.
- H3: Can we speak to a current client before signing?
- P: Yes. After the audit we will connect you with a client in a comparable category and at a comparable stage. We do not screen the conversation.
