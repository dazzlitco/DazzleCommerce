Creative. Filter mapping used in the clone (inferred from the DOM order above):

PPC → PPC Specialist; Development → empty state; Creative → empty state;

## Section 6 — Our Hiring Process

- Eyebrow: `Our Hiring Process`
- H2: `Six steps, roughly three weeks`
- Intro: `No mystery and no ghosting. You will always know which stage you are at and what happens next.`
- Six numbered steps (H6 number + H3 + body):

1. `01` — `Apply` — `A short form and your CV. No cover letter, no 40-field application portal, no account to create.`
2. `02` — `Application Review` — `A human on the hiring team reads it within five working days. You get an answer either way — silence is not a response we use.`
3. `03` — `Interview` — `Forty-five minutes with the person who would manage you. Mostly about how you think and what you have actually shipped.`
4. `04` — `Skills Assessment` — `A short, paid, realistic task — an audit or a plan for an anonymised account. Never spec work on a live client.`
5. `05` — `Offer` — `Salary band shared up front, written offer, and a call to walk through the progression framework before you decide.`
6. `06` — `Welcome Aboard` — `A structured 30-day onboarding with a named buddy, and no client-facing ownership until you say you are ready.`
