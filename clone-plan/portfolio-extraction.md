## 5. Project Spotlight — Nutriblend

- Eyebrow: `Project Spotlight`
- H2: `Nutriblend: turning repeat intent into recurring revenue`
- Subline: `Health & supplements · Shopify Plus · Seven-month engagement, still running`
- Spotlight image: reuses `cases-Nutriblend.webp` — alt: `Dazzle Commerce SEO team working through category page structure`
- Stats:
  - `+214%` — `Revenue YoY` — `Against a $4.1M baseline`
  - `46%` — `Subscription rate` — `Up from 2% of orders`
  - `-31%` — `Blended CAC` — `Across all paid channels`

### 01 — The brief

`Nutriblend came to us with $4.1M in annual revenue, a 3.2x blended ROAS and a repeat rate of 18% on a product category where customers physically run out every thirty days. Their subscription option existed but converted at under 2% because it sat below the fold in a dropdown.`

### 02 — What we found

`Ninety-one percent of second orders were placed manually between day 26 and day 38. The demand for replenishment was already there; the store simply was not asking for it. Paid media was also spending 84% of budget on cold prospecting for a product with strong organic repeat intent.`

### 03 — What we shipped

`A rebuilt PDP with subscribe-and-save as the default selection and a visible per-unit saving. A post-purchase flow that offered conversion to subscription at day 21. A paid structure split between prospecting, win-back and subscription-lapse audiences with separate targets for each.`

### 04 — What happened

`Subscription rate moved from 2% to 46% of orders over seven months. Blended CAC fell 31% because win-back audiences converted at a fraction of prospecting cost. Revenue grew 214% year on year, with 58% of it now recurring.`

### 05 — What we would change

`We rolled the PDP change out to 100% of traffic rather than testing it, because the qualitative signal was strong and the deadline was a product launch. It worked, but we could not isolate how much of the lift came from the layout versus the pricing display. On the next account we split it.`
