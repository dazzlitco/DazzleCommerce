### 10. FAQs — eCommerce SEO FAQs
- Eyebrow/label: `FAQs`
- H2: `eCommerce SEO FAQs`
- Intro: `Eight questions that come up before most SEO engagements. More on our full FAQ page .` (link text `our full FAQ page` → /faqs/ ; note the space before the period in the live copy, kept verbatim)
- Quirk: the intro says "Eight questions" but the live page actually lists NINE questions (also 9 in the JSON-LD FAQPage). All nine are kept verbatim below.
- 9 FAQs:
  1. Q: `How long does eCommerce SEO take to work?` A: `Technical fixes and on-page work on existing ranking pages often show inside 30–90 days. New category and content targets typically take 4–9 months to reach their position, longer in highly competitive categories or on a domain with little authority. Anyone quoting you a fixed timeline before seeing your Search Console data is guessing.`
  2. Q: `What is different about SEO for an online store?` A: `Scale and structure. A store has thousands of URLs generated by filters, variants and pagination, which creates index bloat and crawl waste that content sites never face. Category and collection pages — not blog posts — carry most non-brand revenue. And the same product data has to work for Google, Amazon and your own on-site search at once.`
  3. Q: `Do you write product descriptions for the whole catalog?` A: `At scale, yes — but templated and prioritised rather than hand-writing ten thousand pages. We rewrite the pages that carry revenue or have demand behind them properly, and use structured templates driven by your attribute data for the long tail. Where a catalog genuinely needs full rewriting we price that separately and tell you honestly what it costs.`
  4. Q: `Will SEO replace our paid search spend?` A: `It should reduce it, not replace it. Once organic owns a cluster you can pull back paid on those terms and redeploy the budget — we did exactly that on one engagement and cut paid dependency 24%. But paid still covers new products, seasonal peaks and terms organic will never win. Running them together is the point of doing both.`
  5. Q: `What does eCommerce SEO cost?` A: `Standalone SEO engagements typically run $2,500–$8,000/month depending on catalog size, competitiveness and whether content production is in scope. Inside a full management engagement it is part of the wider fee. Our pricing page explains how we scope it.` (links: `management engagement` → /ecommerce-management/ ; `Our pricing page` → /pricing/)
  6. Q: `Can you handle a replatform without losing rankings?` A: `Yes, and it is planning rather than luck. A full URL inventory, a mapped redirect for every URL that has earned a link or a ranking, preserved patterns where they carry equity, and a staged crawl-and-fix programme for the first eight weeks after launch. On the last one we retained 94% of rankings.`
  7. Q: `Do you build links?` A: `Where the category needs authority to compete, yes — through digital PR, supplier and partner relationships, and content genuinely worth citing. We do not buy links or use private networks. If your gap is technical or structural rather than authority, we will tell you that instead of selling you links you do not need.`
  8. Q: `How do you report on SEO?` A: `Monthly, on non-brand sessions and revenue segmented by page group — categories, products, content — against the pre-engagement baseline. Rankings and visibility scores are included as diagnostics. The report also lists what shipped that month and what did not work.`
  9. Q: `Should you hire an eCommerce SEO agency, an eCommerce SEO expert, or build in-house?` A: `Three different things, and the right answer depends on what is already in place. An eCommerce SEO expert working solo is the cheapest way to get a diagnosis and a plan, and the slowest way to ship template changes across ten thousand URLs. An eCommerce SEO company with a production team behind it is the opposite. In-house works once someone owns the roadmap full-time and you have development capacity to feed. We take the agency side of that on the assumption your developers are already busy — the eCommerce SEO services we run include the implementation, not just the recommendation.`
